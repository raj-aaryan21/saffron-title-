Cooked the way it was taught to us

Saffron Table started as a family kitchen with one rule: nothing comes from a packet. Our masalas are roasted and ground each morning, our breads are rolled to order, and our curries simmer for hours.

We buy vegetables from local farms and dairy from nearby producers, so the menu changes with what is best that week.
